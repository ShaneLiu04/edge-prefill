# AR008 acceptance: combination matrix, routing and the multi-needle fix

Every number comes from one session on the same host (Xeon 6234, 16 threads,
Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct Q4_K_M). Absolute TTFT varies up to ~3x across sessions
on this shared VM (this session ran 1.4-3x slower than the AR007 session on
the same cells), so only same-session ratios are meaningful. Raw samples:
`<dataset>-<combo>/*_raw.csv`, one env JSON per cell.

## 1. The multi-needle "quality failure" was a broken discriminator

`deep/multineedle` asks `What is the secret passcode for vault N?` against a
document that contains `The secret passcode for vault N is <CODE>.` once per
vault. The **only** difference between the three sibling facts is the digit.
The selector's query-term filter was `len >= 2`, so the digit was dropped,
all three blocks scored identically, and the answer was whichever block came
first:

| admission | budget | quality |
|---|---:|---:|
| AR007 selection (`phrase_weight=0`) | 0.05 | **0.333** (2/6) |
| AR008 selection (`phrase_weight=0.5`) | 0.05 | **1.000** (6/6) |

Same budget, same blocks per case (391 tokens), same machine, one signal
added: positional phrases (`vault 5`), matched across block boundaries, and
anchored by the **highest-IDF** phrase rather than by the block's total phrase
score. Cost: 2271 -> 2229 ms at 8K and 4826 -> 4907 ms at 16K, i.e. within
+/-2% (the SRS tolerance), with quality unchanged at 1.000 on both single-needle
sets.

Two sub-findings that cost a round each and are now regression-tested:

- Anchoring on the block with the highest *summed* phrase score loses to a
  template phrase (`for vault` is present in all three sibling blocks). The
  winner must be the highest-IDF phrase.
- A needle sentence routinely starts in block b and ends in b+1. Per-block
  bigrams then miss `vault 7` entirely; the crossing pair (last word of b +
  first word of b+1) has to be credited to both blocks.

| dataset | dense | selection | gate-p16 | gate-p24-local32 | routed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 4K | 66.3 s | **5.5 s (12.0x), q=1.00** | 18.5 s (3.6x) | 14.5 s (4.6x) | selection |
| 8K | 141.9 s | **5.7 s (24.9x), q=1.00** | 25.7 s (5.5x) | 25.1 s (5.6x) | selection |
| 16K | 302.7 s | **15.9 s (19.1x), q=1.00** | 57.3 s (5.3x) | 60.1 s (5.0x) | selection |
| 32K | 852.1 s | 23.1 s (36.8x), **q=0.875** | 63.8 s (13.4x), q=0.25 | **66.8 s (12.8x), q=1.00** | gate-p24-local32 |
| multi-needle | 92.0 s | **8.2 s (11.2x), q=1.00** | 38.2 s (2.4x) | 16.7 s (5.5x) | selection |

The routing rule is the SRS one: fastest combination whose mean **and** min
quality reach 0.95, else the highest quality. That yields selection everywhere
except 32K, where selection misses the floor (0.875) and the gate holds it
(1.000) for 2.9x more TTFT. The gate is *not* free quality: at 8K/16K it is
4-6x slower than selection for the same quality, which is exactly why a fixed
"always gate" policy would have thrown away the AR007 speedups.

## 4. Defect found by the matrix (fixed, regression-tested)

The router's first version read `qg_bound`/`qg_sel_pages` from the per-cell
`Config`, but those knobs live on `BenchmarkConfig`. The gate arms therefore
ran **without the gate** (`qg_source` empty, 16K quality 0.356 = the pure
selection number) while reporting gate latency. The matrix caught it because
it records `qg_source` per cell. After the fix: 4K gate q=1.00/18.5 s,
8K q=1.00/25.7 s, 16K q=1.00/57.3 s, 32K p24+local32 q=1.00/66.8 s. The lesson
is recorded in design.md §10 and the audit columns are part of every cell.

## 5. What AR008 does not claim

- The portfolio table is **model- and dataset-bound**: it carries the model
  SHA256 and is regenerated by measurement (`./repro.sh ar008`). It is opt-in
  (`portfolio` in the conf); without it nothing changes.
- The 32K decision buys quality with latency (12.8x instead of 36.8x). That is
  a measured trade-off, not a free win; a user who prefers speed can keep
  `selection` and accept q=0.875.
- The phrase signal cannot help prompts with no discriminative phrase
  (`phrase_blocks=0`), e.g. purely semantic or paraphrased queries. For those
  prompts the profile falls into the `flat` bucket and the table's `default`
  row applies - no claim is made that routing improves them, because no
  dataset in the repository measures that regime.
- Absolute numbers on a CUDA host are unmeasured (no GPU here).
